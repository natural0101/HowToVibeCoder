## 2. Universal Global HR Satire Mechanic — «HR Вайб-Чек» (`Vibe Check`)
One unified mechanic across ALL languages (RU, EN, and future ZH/DE/ES/PT/FR/JA/KO/PL) with ZERO region-branching logic:
- HR refuses to read real code and evaluates every captured vibe coder using an absurd unified **Vibe Check** combining three globally recognizable badges:
  1. **Tarot Card** (visual desk prop + instant comedic icon: *The Tower, The Fool, Wheel of Fortune*)
  2. **MBTI Code** (`INFP`, `ENTJ`, `ENFP`, `INTJ` — universal 4-letter code understood worldwide from US/EU to Korea/China/Japan!)
  3. **Cosmic / Culture-Fit Verdict** (*Mercury in retrograde*, *Culture Fit 12%*, *Cheap Aura*)

## 3. Master Narrative Arc — Super-Cunning Mastermind HR Veronika (Option 1 Canon!)
- **Floor 1 (Beloved Ally with Subtle Foreshadowing):**
  - On the surface, **HR Veronika** is the player's charismatic, witty best ally: teaches the ropes, buys catches, hands out 90s arsenal from her velvet briefcase, spins skins on her slot-machine head. Players fall in love with her!
  - **Subtle Red Flags planted on Floor 1 (visible only in hindsight):**
    * Beneath the burgundy velvet of her Tarot desk hums a hidden `SYSTEM ZERO` prediction API terminal.
    * She was the one who originally hired the entire horde of vibe coders by Tarot & MBTI while the Boss was away.
    * She calmly sells the Boss the cloned North Elevator keycard, nudging him to unleash **AI Agent v0.1** and clear the path upward.
    * In the Floor 1 finale, she smoothly steers the Boss toward Floor 2 to "clean out the useless executives upstairs."
- **Middle Floors (Floors 2, 3, 4... — Rotating Ridiculous HR / Management Mini-Bosses):**
  - Each higher floor introduces a fresh absurd corporate sub-HR / executive (e.g., *Agile Chakra Coach*, *MBTI Culture-Fit Director*, *Prompt-CTO*, *Astrology Legal Lead*) so every floor stays visually and comedically fresh.
  - Veronika stays on the Boss's radio/intercom as his "helpful guide", quietly feeding intel — actually using the Boss's fists to eliminate every single rival executive blocking her climb to the top!
- **Top Floor Penthouse Finale (The Portal/GLaDOS Betrayal Twist):**
  - When the Boss breaches the CEO Penthouse, **Veronika** sits in the CEO throne — fully fused with `SYSTEM ZERO`, wielding a giant gilded Tarot deck and an orbital Vegas slot rig.
  - **The Reveal:** The AI Agent never hijacked Entropic Corp alone — **Veronika orchestrated the entire vibe-coder disaster**, bankrupted the Boss on purpose, and used the Boss as her personal 90s enforcer to wipe out every rival executive floor by floor so she could crown herself Supreme Oracle-CEO!
